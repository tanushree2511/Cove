## Ranking and reasoning
1. INDIACom-2027: only candidate whose own site shows an IEEE section approval and an IEEE conference ID, 6-page limit is explicit, a comfortable three-and-a-half weeks, no AI gate. Weaker topic fit and Xplore is only applied for.
2. ICMDRSE-2027: best topic match and a later deadline (10 Jan), but the 0% AI rule, missing IEEE number and first-edition status make it riskier; only go if the AI rule can be met and organisers confirm IEEE co-sponsorship and 6 pages in writing.
3. COEP CON 2027: longest runway and explicit AI-disclosure route, but sponsorship wording missing; verify first. A realistic fallback if INDIACom fails.
4. SCES-2027: ideal student format, but deadline 15 Nov is tentative and 2027 sponsorship unconfirmed; only if the authors can verify IEEE status and finish within 5 days (or the deadline is extended).

## Pre-submission checklist for the authors
1. Re-read the current CFP on the day of submission; deadlines above have inconsistencies.
2. Look up the conference in IEEE's database (conferences.ieee.org, or the IEEE Conference Record/ID, e.g. 74162 for INDIACom) and confirm name, dates and sponsor match. For COEP CON, ICMDRSE and SCES, ask the organisers for the IEEE number in writing.
3. Confirm the page limit by email for ICMDRSE (6 vs 8).
4. AI-use rule: ICMDRSE demands 0% AI-generated content; COEP CON requires disclosure in Acknowledgments for substantive AI use; INDIACom and SCES state nothing. Decide honestly what was AI-assisted in the text and follow the rule (this project used AI assistance).
5. Anonymise for double-blind venues (INDIACom, ICMDRSE, COEP CON): remove names, college, repository links.
6. Run a similarity check below the venue threshold (ICMDRSE 10%, COEP CON under 20%; INDIACom uses Turnitin, no number).
7. Rejection elsewhere: no page prohibits resubmitting a rejected paper; all require the paper not be under review elsewhere. Withdraw it from any other venue first.
8. Register at least one author before the final-version date; fees are non-refundable at INDIACom. Prepare the IEEE copyright transfer (INDIACom provides a form; COEP CON states transfer to IEEE).
9. Run IEEE PDF eXpress on the final PDF.
