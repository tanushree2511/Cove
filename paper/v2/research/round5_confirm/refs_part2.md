# Reference audit, round 5, part 2 (23 keys)

Date of audit: 2026-11-10. Method: Crossref API (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`), arXiv abstract pages, CVF/NeurIPS/ICLR/ACM/Springer pages where reachable, publisher-independent author pages, SciSpace/Consensus abstract text, WebSearch. dblp.org, Springer chapter pages, IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, OpenReview and the CVF HTML pages returned 403/redirect/Anubis block for automated fetches, so they were not used as primary evidence (secondary evidence is flagged). Descriptions were checked against abstracts (or the first pages of the PDFs where I could parse them); every description check is **abstract-level** unless stated otherwise. Paper-side sentences are quoted from `cove_paper.tex`.

Result: **11 VERIFIED, 12 PARTLY, 0 UNVERIFIED.** Every one of the 23 works exists. No author, title, year or DOI in the bib is wrong. The "PARTLY" verdicts are missing volume/pages/DOI (all 12) and, for 6 of them, a paper sentence that needs rewording (plus one factual error on EdgeFace: it is trained with CosFace, not ArcFace).

## Descriptions to reword in the paper

1. **EdgeFace loss (factual error) and 512-D MobileFaceNets** (`cove_paper.tex` line 126-128).
   Current: "ArcFace~\cite{deng2019arcface} training with compact backbones such as MobileFaceNets, GhostFaceNets and EdgeFace gives accurate 512-D embeddings at a few hundred MFLOPs."
   Problems: EdgeFace is trained with a CosFace head; the original MobileFaceNets paper outputs 128-D (221 M MAdds); 512-D is true for EdgeFace, GhostFaceNets, and the InsightFace MBF used here.
   Suggested: "Margin-based softmax losses such as ArcFace~\cite{deng2019arcface} (CosFace for EdgeFace) allow compact backbones such as MobileFaceNets~\cite{chen2018mobilefacenets}, GhostFaceNets~\cite{alansari2023ghostfacenets} and EdgeFace~\cite{george2024edgeface} to give accurate embeddings (512-D in the models we use) at roughly 0.06 to 0.5 GFLOPs."
   (GhostFaceNets "as low as 61M FLOPs" is quoted in the EdgeFace paper; EdgeFace base widths 94.7 to 461.7 MFLOPs; MobileFaceNet 221 M MAdds.)

2. **Daniels threshold tuning** (line 112): "Constraints can also be used to tune a clusterer's thresholds~\cite{daniels2006threshold,vancraenendonck2017selection}."
   Suggested: "A few labelled examples~\cite{daniels2006threshold} or constraints~\cite{vancraenendonck2017selection} can also be used to tune a clusterer's threshold or hyperparameters."

3. **Biswas and Jacobs** (lines 118-119): "Active face clustering with human answers~\cite{biswas2014active} is the usual frame for simulated users."
   Suggested: "Active image clustering, where a person answers pairwise same/different questions chosen by the algorithm~\cite{biswas2014active}, is the closest frame for our simulated users."

4. **Song and Leung** (line 116-117): "Photo album systems have long let users label or correct face groups and propagate the labels~\cite{tian2007face,cui2007easyalbum,song2006context}".
   Suggested: move `song2006context` out of the "label or correct" group: "...~\cite{tian2007face,cui2007easyalbum}, and consumer-photo person clustering has used clothes and time context with hard constraints from logic cues and user feedback~\cite{song2006context}".

5. **Kleppmann et al.** (line 119-121): "...is the idea behind conflict-free replicated data types~\cite{shapiro2011crdt} and local-first software~\cite{kleppmann2019local}".
   Suggested: "...is the idea behind conflict-free replicated data types~\cite{shapiro2011crdt}, which local-first software~\cite{kleppmann2019local} proposes as a foundation for apps that keep the primary copy of the data on the user's device."

Other descriptions (davidson2006utility, zhu2016imperfect, mazumdar2017noisy, vancraenendonck2017selection, shapiro2011crdt, shen2020backward, guo2022scrfd, johnson2021faiss, the dataset and metric citations) are accurate at abstract level. Minor optional wording: write "SCRFD-0.5GF (InsightFace SCRFD_500M)" once, because the SCRFD paper itself does not use the name "500M".
